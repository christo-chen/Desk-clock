隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 7 日

一句话：桌面主题时钟不收集、不上传、不分享你的任何数据。

我们收集哪些数据

不收集任何数据。App 没有账号系统，不包含统计分析、广告或第三方 SDK，运行时也不连接网络。

保存在你设备上的内容

你选择的主题、颜色、时钟样式等设置，以及你自己添加的语录，只保存在你的 iPhone 或 iPad 上。它们不会离开你的设备，删除 App 时会一并删除。

使用到的系统功能

  • 电量：用于在屏幕上显示电池图标，只在本机读取，不会记录或发送。
  • 屏幕常亮：防止时钟显示时屏幕自动锁定，不涉及任何个人数据。

儿童隐私

由于不收集任何数据，本 App 同样不会收集儿童的个人信息。

政策变更

如果以后的版本改变了数据处理方式（例如加入广告），会先更新本页面并修改上方的生效日期。

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 7 November 2026

In short: Desk Clock does not collect, upload or share any of your data.

Data we collect

None. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no third-party SDKs, and it does not connect to the internet while running.

What stays on your device

Your settings (theme, colours, clock style and so on) and any quotes you add are stored only on your iPhone or iPad. They never leave your device and are removed when you delete the app.

System features the app uses

  • Battery level: read on the device only, to draw the battery icon. It is never recorded or sent anywhere.
  • Keep screen on: stops the screen from locking while the clock is shown. No personal data is involved.

Children's privacy

Because the app collects no data at all, it collects no personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version changes how data is handled (for example, by adding ads), this page will be updated first and the effective date above will change.
